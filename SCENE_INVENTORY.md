## Interaction plan

- A single locked 16:9 scene is shown at a time on presentation displays.
- Keyboard, clicker-compatible keys, on-screen controls, direct overview navigation and fullscreen are supported.
- Staggered reveals follow the spoken order on the history, five-day, and three-project scenes.
- Scene 6 deliberately pauses ambient motion; scene 8 shifts emphasis to IOOS; scene 12 collapses the old toolchain visually.
- Presenter mode is opened with `P`, stores private notes locally, and remains hidden from audience fullscreen.
- Phones and tablets switch to a readable vertical layout; diagrams can be tapped to open at full size.

## Extraction limits

- The external Ocean Evidence Commons application cannot function offline and its availability is outside this project. The scene includes an authentic static fallback and an “Open live demo” link.
- Montserrat is represented through a system-first Montserrat/Avenir stack so the static build does not depend on a remote font service.
- All visible narrative copy, screenshots, diagrams, branding and the 15-scene sequence were extracted faithfully from v9.
